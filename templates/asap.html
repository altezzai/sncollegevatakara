Additional Skill Acquisition Programme (ASAP)

Additional Skill Acquisition Programme (ASAP) Kerala is a State Public Sector Undertaking fully owned by the Government of Kerala under the Higher Education Department and incorporated under Section 8 of The Companies Act, 2013. It focuses on skilling students and the general community to enhance their employability.

Sree Narayana College, Vatakara has made an MoU with ASAP Kerala and became an ASAP skill development center in Vatakara. We provide 60+ courses which include both offline and online options as add-on and internship courses.
