The Campuses Of Kozhikode (COK) unit has been functioning in Sree Narayana College since 2023. The unit organizes many programs that help in the overall development of students.

It mainly focuses on developing students' personalities through community services, promoting social welfare, and providing services to society without any bias.

Key Activities

The COK unit is actively involved in various social service initiatives, such as:

  • Blood donation camps
  • Cleaning drives
  • Charity activities
  • Community service programs
