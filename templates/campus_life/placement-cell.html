The Placement and Career Cell of Sree Narayana College, Vadakara, plays a vital role in guiding students toward successful career opportunities. The cell actively works to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements by organizing various training programs, workshops, and placement drives.

The overall functioning of the Placement and Career Cell significantly contributes to the professional growth and future readiness of the students, enhancing their employability and preparing them for successful careers.

Key Initiatives & Achievements
Skill Development

Throughout the academic year, the cell conducts several activities such as career guidance sessions, aptitude training, soft skill development programs, and mock interviews to help students prepare for competitive recruitment processes.

Campus Recruitment

The cell has established connections with various companies and organizations to facilitate campus recruitment drives. As a result of these consistent efforts, approximately 20% of the students were successfully placed.

Higher Studies & Entrepreneurship

In addition to direct placements, the cell actively encourages students to pursue higher studies and entrepreneurial ventures by providing relevant information, guidance, and support.

Placement Cell Team

The cell is efficiently coordinated by Ms. Divya Subhash, with the support of Ms. Jipsy Rani as Assistant Coordinator, and Ms. Reshma R as a dedicated member. Their collective efforts ensure smooth functioning and effective engagement with students and recruiters.
