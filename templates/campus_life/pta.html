The Parents Teachers Association (PTA) of our college plays a vital role in supporting students and contributing to the overall development of the institution. The PTA continues to be a strong pillar of support, working towards the welfare of students and the progressive development of the college.

Executive Committee

All decisions regarding PTA activities and fund utilization are taken by the executive committee, which consists of 11 members. The principal will be the president of the PTA, and the vice president will be from the parents' side.

General Body Meeting

The general body meeting is convened every year in March to review activities, present accounts, and plan future initiatives.

Major Activities
Scholarship & Financial Assistance

The PTA provides financial aid and scholarships to financially backward students to ensure uninterrupted education.

Infrastructure & Development

The association regularly contributes to the developmental activities and infrastructure of the college.

Academic & Skill Development

The PTA actively supports various academic and skill development activities for the students.
