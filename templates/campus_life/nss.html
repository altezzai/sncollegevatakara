Sree Narayana College Vatakara has had an NSS unit since 2004. The NSS unit is involved in various activities like blood donation camps, AIDS awareness programmes, charity activities and community services.

The College's NSS unit focuses on developing student's personalities through community service, promoting social welfare and providing services without bias. The unit also organizes a seven day residential camp focused on community development.

Organizing residential camps and various social service initiatives.
