Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of Sree Narayana College, Vadakara was constituted on 6th October 2023 as a proactive step towards achieving academic excellence and preparing for accreditation by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). The formation of IQAC reflects the institution's commitment to quality enhancement, continuous improvement, and systematic performance evaluation in all academic and administrative activities.

Vision

To promote a culture of quality consciousness, innovation, and institutional excellence through structured and sustained quality initiatives.

Objectives
  • To develop a system for conscious, consistent, and catalytic action to improve academic and administrative performance.
  • To prepare the institution for NAAC accreditation.
  • To promote best practices in teaching, learning, research and extension activities.
  • To ensure timely documentation and reporting quality initiatives.
Location

Sree Narayana College, Vadakara, a Self Financing college, is nestled atop a lush, green hill, in a remote village, of Maniyur Grama Panchayath in the Kozhikode district of Kerala, affiliated to University of Calicut. The college is situated at approximately 11.60° N latitude and 75.58° E longitude.

Academic Programmes

The College offers 14 undergraduate and 5 post graduate programmes in Arts, Science, Commerce and Management. From the 2024-25 academic year onwards, the college offers innovative, flexible, skill-oriented, and multidisciplinary 'Four Year Undergraduate Programmes (FYUGP)' designed to enhance learning and career prospects.

Activities & Initiatives
NAAC Workshop
5th Dec 2023

One-day workshop on "NAAC, an Overview of Revised Accreditation Guidelines" was organized to familiarize faculty members and administrative staff with NAAC criteria, metrics documentation, data management, and institutional preparedness strategies.

Envision - 2024
22nd - 29th Jan 2024

An enriching series of seminars and workshops coordinated by various departments, embracing a wide range of topics and expertise to provide valuable insights and foster knowledge exchange.

Orientation Programme on FYUGP
2024 • Town Hall

Organized for the general public and prospective students. Dr. K.T. Varghese delivered an insightful presentation explaining the curriculum framework, credit structure, and multiple exit options under the new FYUGP system.

Online Orientation on FYUGP
28th Jun 2024

Conducted under the leadership of the Honorable Minister of Higher Education, Kerala, for the faculties of higher education institutions. Emphasized the importance of revamping higher education to achieve global standards.

Envision - 2025
10th - 17th Feb 2025

An intellectual journey through 11 technical sessions that ignited curiosity among participants, elevated the understanding of present scenarios of relevant subjects, and empowered students through dynamic interactions.

Vijnanolsavam
1st Jul 2025

Inauguration of the FYUGP for 2025-26, inaugurated by Hon. Higher Education Minister Dr. R. Bindu. The program was live-streamed, followed by an orientation lecture by the Academic Coordinator outlining academic opportunities.

Envision - 2026
11th & 13th Feb 2026

Envisioned as a platform to enrich knowledge and encourage academic interaction among students and faculty alike. Through such programs, IQAC strives to enhance quality, innovation, and excellence in higher education.
