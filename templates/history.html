Overview & History

Established in 2003 by the SNDP Union Development and Welfare Society, Vadakara, Sree Narayana College is a self-financing institution located at Keezhal, Kozhikode District. Nestled atop a lush, green hill, the college stands as a beacon of academic excellence and values inspired by the teachings of Sree Narayana Guru.

The goodwill of our institution extends beyond academic achievements, embracing a legacy of values, a beautiful campus setting, and faculties devoted to shaping the leaders of tomorrow. The college prides itself on fostering the holistic development of students, instilling a sense of social responsibility and ethical conduct.

Affiliations & Approvals
  • Affiliated with the University of Calicut.
  • Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).
  • Recognized by the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC), reflecting a commitment to quality education, innovation, and academic excellence.
Academic Programmes

We offer a comprehensive education with a diverse array of 14 undergraduate and 5 post-graduate programmes spanning Arts, Science, Commerce, and Management.

In alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, the college successfully introduced the Four Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUGP) structure in 2024, providing multidisciplinary exposure, academic flexibility, skill enhancement, and research orientation.

Infrastructure

The campus is equipped with well-maintained infrastructure, spacious classrooms, a well-stocked library, modern laboratories, computer facilities, and ICT-enabled learning resources to support effective teaching-learning processes.

Campus Life & Activities
  • NSS Unit: A vibrant National Service Scheme unit that instills social commitment, leadership qualities, and community engagement.
  • Sports & Games: A strong tradition of excellence, with our men's and women's volleyball teams proudly representing the institution at the national level.
  • Arts & Culture: Active participation is encouraged, with students consistently achieving recognition at the University level.
Campus Environment

The institution strictly enforces a zero-tolerance policy and maintains a student-friendly campus atmosphere in accordance with UGC norms and government regulations.
