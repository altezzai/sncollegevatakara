Vision & Mission
Vision

To evolve into a centre of excellence in higher education by fostering academic innovation, holistic development, and ethical values, while responding effectively to the emerging educational and societal needs.

Mission
  • To provide quality education through modern teaching-learning practices.
  • To strengthen research culture and promote interdisciplinary learning.
  • To enhance student support services and ensure inclusive education.
  • To upgrade infrastructure and technology to meet global standards.
  • To build strong industry-academia linkages and community engagement.
