Funding Agencies (Management)
Funding Body

Sree Narayana College, Vatakara is a Self Financing Institution established and operated by the SNDP Union Development and Welfare Society, Vatkara, that do not receive regular financial aid or maintenance grants from the government.

This institution is primarily self funded relying on tuition fees and management contributions to cover expenses for infrastructure, staff salaries, and operational costs.

Funding Sources
Fees

Tuition fees, Admission fee and Lab and library fees are the primary revenue source, covering salaries, maintenance and overheads.

Development Fee & Donations

Management collects development funds and donations, especially under management seats.

Alumni Funds

Funds generated from alumni may be used for the welfare and development of students.

PTA Fund

Used for the welfare of the students and for granting aid for poor and meritorious students.

Government & University Contributions

Funds are also received for the extension activities of NSS and other clubs from the University and state government.
