Administrative Office

The College Administrative Office plays a vital role in the smooth and efficient functioning of the institution. It serves as the central hub for managing all administrative, academic, and official activities of the college.

It ensures proper documentation and timely communication between the management, faculty, students, and external authorities. The staff of the administrative office are committed to providing prompt and reliable services to students and faculty, ensuring transparency and efficiency in all operations.

Modern record-keeping practices and systematic procedures are followed to maintain accuracy and accountability.

Key Responsibilities
  • Maintaining student records, processing admissions, handling correspondence, and supporting the conduct of examinations.
  • Managing financial transactions, fee collection, staff records, and the implementation of institutional policies.
  • Working in close coordination with various departments and committees to ensure the effective execution of academic and administrative plans.
